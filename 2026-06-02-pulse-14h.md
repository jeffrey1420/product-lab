## Signal 2: Agentic AI — The Spectrum Is Being Formalized

The concept of "agentic AI" is being systematically defined, moving beyond marketing hype toward measurable criteria.

### The Agentic Spectrum (from agentic.ai, updated June 2, 2026)
Based on 282 tools scored across 32 categories, agentic systems are mapped across **six levels**:
1. **Reactive Tool** — single prompt, single response
2. **Tool User** — can call tools but no loop
3. **Single-Turn Agent** — decides + acts + observes once
4. **Loop Agent** — full ReAct loop (decide-act-observe-repeat)
5. **Multi-Agent Coordinator** — coordinates multiple agents
6. **Strategic Agent** — long-horizon planning with goal decomposition

Most enterprise AI agent products today sit at levels 3-4. Opus 4.8's benchmarks suggest frontier models are now reliable at level 4 for complex, long-running tasks.

### Inria's Framing (March 23, 2026)
France's national research institute published a substantive piece on agentic AI, noting it represents a **functional and systemic rupture** rather than a scientific breakthrough. Key points:
- Users shift from "rédige un mail" to "gère mes mails cette semaine"
- Autonomy in action (not just information access) is the defining characteristic
- Research applications already live: AI Scientist v2 (April 2026) can execute end-to-end complex research tasks — hypothesis, code, results, paper formatting
- Major ethical and governance challenges: responsibility, transparency, traceability, reproducibility

### Implication for Alizé
The formalization of the agentic spectrum is Alizé's product framing opportunity. Alizé can position itself as the **governance layer for levels 4-6** — ensuring that as enterprises deploy more autonomous agents, there's a compliance, oversight, and control structure in place. The EU AI Act's human oversight requirements (Article 14) map directly to this need.
