**Six levels of agenticness (0–32 score):**
- L0 Reactive Tool (0–5): Chatbots, static Q&A
- L1 Guided Assistant (6–11): Single-step task execution
- L2 Adaptive Collaborator (12–17): Multi-step plans with human approval
- L3 Domain Specialist (18–23): Domain-specific workflows, dynamic replanning
- L4 Autonomous Operator (24–28): Cross-domain workflows, self-correction
- L5 Strategic Agent (29–32): Fully self-directed, continuous operation

**Strategic insight:** Alizé's target service level is L2–L3. This is intentional — L4+ autonomy in regulated French PME environments creates compliance risk, unpredictability, and CNIL exposure. The positioning message is: "We keep you at the right level of AI autonomy — useful enough to save real time, controlled enough to stay compliant."

## EU AI Act Countdown

**~59 days remaining until August 2, 2026** (high-risk AI system prohibition takes effect).

French enterprises operating AI in Annex I critical infrastructure (energy, transport, healthcare, finance, water, public administration) face **market withdrawal** if non-compliant. The conformity assessment and human oversight documentation requirements are now urgent.

**Key obligations for critical infra AI:**
- Article 12: Technical documentation ( conformity assessment)
- Article 13: Logging and audit trail requirements
- Article 14: Human oversight — "meaningful human control" standard
- Article 15: Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity
- Article 16: Incident reporting (serious incidents within 48h)

This is Alizé's primary commercial window. French AI adopters in critical sectors are now **legally motivated** to seek managed AI services with compliance documentation.
