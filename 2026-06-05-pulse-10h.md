## New Signal 1: Anthropic Calls for Coordinated AI Pause

**Source:** Anthropic Institute, published June 4, 2026

Jack Clark (co-founder) and Anthropic Institute published a paper proposing a global coordination mechanism for AI development. Key points:

- **Core argument:** "It would be good for the world to have the option to slow down or temporarily suspend frontier AI development" to allow societal structures and alignment research to keep pace
- **Metaphor:** "The AI sector has an accelerator pedal but no brake pedal"
- **Implication:** Anthropic is openly acknowledging that competitive pressure is pushing the industry beyond safe development speeds
- **Political resistance expected:** US officials and most tech leaders reject any slowdown that could advantage China

**Why this matters for Alizé:**

This is a validation of Alizé's core premise — that AI deployment needs governance and oversight mechanisms. If Anthropic itself is saying the race is too fast and the industry needs a brake, then enterprise-grade governance isn't just nice-to-have. It's a market necessity.

Alizé's positioning as "AI workflow governance" directly addresses the concern Anthropic is raising: without external oversight, competitive pressure will override safety. Alizé can be the institutional brake — not pausing development, but ensuring enterprise deployments are auditable, constrained, and compliant.

**Takeaway:** Use this narrative. Alizé is not anti-AI; it's the mechanism that makes AI deployment sustainable in regulated, high-stakes environments.

## EU AI Act Countdown

**~57 days remaining until August 2, 2026** (high-risk AI system prohibition takes effect).

France is actively preparing. The CNIL and BPI are running EU AI Act compliance workshops for French companies. Any French or EU enterprise deploying high-risk AI systems (HR, finance, critical infrastructure) after August 2 must demonstrate compliance with Articles 9 (risk management), 10 (data governance), 11 (data usage minimization), and 12 (technical robustness).

**The opening for Alizé:** Companies deploying Vibe Work, Microsoft Agent 365, or any AI agent in high-risk contexts need documentation that:
- The agent accessed only data necessary for its task (Article 11)
- The agent's decisions are explainable and appealable (Article 14)
- There's an audit trail of what the agent did and when (Article 12)

Alizé produces this documentation automatically.
