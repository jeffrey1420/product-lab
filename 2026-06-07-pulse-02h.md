## Finding 2: Snowflake Summit Governance Article — The Full Picture

**Source:** Maddyness, June 4, 2026 (full article translation)

The June 6 evening pulse noted the Snowflake Summit findings in summary form. The full Maddyness article adds critical nuance:

**Key quote (paraphrased):**
> "An agent that summarizes a meeting is comfortable. An agent that acts inside Salesforce, Workday, Slack, Jira or an ERP — that is political. It doesn't just help: it triggers, modifies, prioritizes, recommends, and sometimes decides."

**Three-layer framework from the article:**

**Layer 1 — The technical shift:** AI moving from "assistance" to "action." This is now explicitly narrated by Snowflake + Accenture + Anthropic + Sanofi together. The market has aligned on this characterization.

**Layer 2 — The organizational blind spot:** 
> "L'agentique va forcer les entreprises à expliciter leurs règles internes. Et beaucoup découvriront que ces règles n'existent pas vraiment, ou qu'elles reposent sur des compromis politiques plus que sur une gouvernance assumée."

This is precisely the "workflow readiness" gap that Alizé's Workflow Readiness Score (WRS) instrument is designed to surface. Companies that haven't documented their internal rules will be forced to — and many will discover they have nothing written down.

**Layer 3 — The power shift:**
> "Demain, celui qui maîtrise la donnée, les accès et les workflows ne sera plus une fonction support. Il tiendra une partie de la chaîne de commandement de l'entreprise automatisée."

Context masters will hold power inside automated enterprises. This is the "context governance" thesis in French industry language.

**Anthropic IPO context:** Daniela Amodei (Anthropic president) appeared at Snowflake hours after Anthropic filed IPO preparatory documents. Her speech on trust and security reads differently in that context — governance is not just an ethical position, it's a commercial and financial one. Public markets will price governance capability.
