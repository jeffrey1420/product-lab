## Signal #1: Anthropic Files for IPO — $965B Valuation, $47B ARR

### What Happened

Anthropic officially filed a confidential S-1 with the SEC on June 1, 2026, confirmed June 2 by multiple French and US news outlets (Clubic, Euronews, Le Monde). This is the first major AI company to file for IPO ahead of OpenAI.

**Key numbers:**
- **Valuation:** $965B / ~€840B (after $65B raise)
- **Annualized revenue:** $47B (up from $9B end of 2025) — 5x growth in ~18 months
- **IPO timing:** Possible as early as autumn 2026, pending SEC review
- **Claude Code** driving developer adoption; **Claude Cowork** (Mac AI agent) expanding consumer/prosumer reach

**What this means for the AI market:**
OpenAI is expected to file next. SpaceX + xAI merger is also going public at ~$1.25T valuation. The entire AI sector is about to get validated (or repriced) by public market scrutiny. Every enterprise AI vendor will be compared to Anthropic's revenue multiple.

### Why This Matters for Alizé

**Anthropic going public changes the enterprise AI compliance conversation in two ways:**

1. **More French enterprises will deploy Claude.** Anthropic's Paris office (opened November 2025) + Mythos EU availability + IPO marketing will drive French enterprise adoption. Every French company that deploys Claude in a regulated context (critical infrastructure, finance, healthcare, transport) still needs EU AI Act conformity assessment, Article 14 human oversight, Article 72 serious incident reporting. Claude being "made in America" doesn't exempt it from EU law.

2. **Anthropic's IPO creates a compliance urgency halo.** When a company goes public, its legal and compliance teams become very sensitive to regulatory risk. Any French enterprise using Claude without proper EU AI Act documentation becomes a liability on Anthropic's books — and Anthropic's legal team will want to push compliance responsibility downstream. This is a sales angle: "Your AI vendor is going public. Their lawyers will want proof you're compliant. We provide that proof."

**The pitch angle for Alizé:** "Anthropic just filed to go public. Their compliance team will be auditing their enterprise customers. You need EU AI Act documentation for every high-risk AI deployment. We provide it."

## Signal #2: Anthropic Mythos Now Accessible to EU Enterprises

France24 reported June 2, 2026 that Anthropic's Mythos model — positioned as a specialist coding and professional task model — is now accessible in the EU. This follows Mythos Glasswing's focus on enterprise vulnerability analysis and the ENISA partnership announced in prior cycles.

**Implication:** The "Anthropic for French enterprise" motion is now complete: Paris office + Mythos EU + IPO filing. Anthropic is no longer a foreign AI vendor — it's positioning as a French-market enterprise player.

**The compliance gap remains.** Mythos deployment in French critical infrastructure requires:
- EU AI Act conformity assessment (Annex III classification)
- Article 14 human oversight documentation
- Article 72 serious incident reporting protocol
- CNIL data processing documentation if personal data is involved

None of this comes from Anthropic. It comes from Alizé.

The GNT and Fnac articles confirm Mistral's AI NOW Summit announcement included a **VS Code extension** for Vibe Code mode, explicitly referencing "vibe coding" — the practice of letting AI write and manage code autonomously.

**What this signals:**

This is a niche Alizé could fill as the market matures — but it's downstream from the immediate workflow compliance opportunity.

**Immediate Alizé angle:** "You're using Vibe Work for automation. Are your AI workflows documented for EU AI Act compliance? If not, your vendor relationship with Mistral doesn't protect you."
