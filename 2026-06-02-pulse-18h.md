## Signal 2: Agentic.ai Formalizes 36-Point Scoring (Updated June 2, 2026)

agentic.ai updated its framework today with a **36-point score across 9 dimensions** (up from 32 categories previously). This is the most rigorous attempt to date to make "agentic" measurable rather than marketing.

### The 9 Dimensions (new)
1. Tool use breadth
2. Loop autonomy
3. Memory persistence
4. Planning depth
5. Error recovery
6. Multi-agent coordination
7. Long-horizon goal tracking
8. Human intervention hooks
9. Output auditability

Dimension 9 ("output auditability") is new and directly relevant to Alizé's compliance positioning — it measures whether an agent's decisions and actions can be traced, explained, and audited. This is precisely what EU AI Act Article 11/14 requires.

**Strategic signal:** As measurement frameworks standardize, enterprise procurement will start requiring "agentic scores" in RFPs. Alizé should track this and consider whether to build scoring tooling or certifications into its offering.

## The Governance Gap — Narrowing but Not Closed

Mistral Vibe's launch actually validates Alizé's core thesis: **the market needs agent platforms**. The more agent platforms exist (Vibe, Claude, Manus, Microsoft), the larger the governance surface.

**What Vibe Work doesn't solve for regulated French enterprises:**
- Which AI systems process what data (Article 11 inventory)
- Who approves agent actions above certain risk thresholds (Article 14 oversight)
- How to demonstrate compliance during an audit
- Cross-platform agent orchestration with unified audit trails

**The closing window:** Mistral will eventually add compliance features to Vibe Enterprise. Alizé's first-mover advantage in French regulated markets is 6-12 months.
