## Finding 1: H Company Joins Nvidia Nemotron — French Computer-Use on the Global Stage

**Source:** Maddyness, June 5, 2026

H Company, a Paris-based AI startup founded in 2023, has joined Nvidia's **Nemotron** coalition — an initiative launched by Nvidia in March 2026 to pool compute, expertise, and data sources for next-generation open-source foundation models. Nemotron's membership roster now includes Black Forest Labs, Cursor, LangChain, Mistral AI, Perplexity, Reflection AI, Sarvam, and Thinking Machines Lab — a notably selective group.

**What H Company does:** H Company specializes in **"computer-use" AI** — a new generation of AI systems that don't just generate text but autonomously execute tasks inside digital environments: browsers, desktop applications, and mobile apps. This is distinct from API-based agents that call tools — computer-use means the AI operates *on* the interfaces themselves, as a human would.

**Key product:** H Company recently launched **Holo3.1**, a computer-use specialized model with competitive benchmark performance at lower cost than generalist models. They also released **Holotab**, a Chrome extension for the general public.

**Scale:** H Company has raised **$220 million**. They employ ~100 people.

**Why this matters for Alizé:** Computer-use agents are the hardest governance problem in AI. When an AI can operate autonomously inside Salesforce, Workday, or any SaaS tool, the question of *what it can access, when, and on whose behalf* becomes acute. This is precisely the "context access governance" layer Alizé is being positioned to own. H Company's existence validates that the computer-use segment is real, funded, and growing — which means the market for governance over computer-use agents will grow proportionally.

## Finding 2: Snowflake Summit 2026 — When AI Agents Stop Assisting and Start Acting

**Source:** Maddyness / Big Data & AI Paris, June 4, 2026

Snowflake's Summit 2026 opened in San Francisco on June 2 with a notable lineup: Sridhar Ramaswamy (Snowflake CEO) on stage alongside Accenture, Anthropic, and Sanofi — a platform company, a consultancy, a model provider, and a major industrial group, all narrating the same shift: **AI moving from assistance to action.**

The core insight from the summit (reported via Maddyness/Big Data & AI Paris coverage):

> "An agent that summarizes a meeting is comfortable. An agent that acts inside Salesforce, Workday, Slack, Jira or an ERP — that is political. It doesn't just help: it triggers, modifies, prioritizes, recommends, and sometimes decides."

**The governance gap this exposes:** As AI agents gain access to operational systems, companies are discovering that their internal rules were never actually explicit. Access rights granted by habit. Processes never documented. Arbitrages between business units and IT that nobody wanted to reopen. Agentic AI is forcing organizations to write down what they thought was understood.

**The Big Data & AI Paris framing (June 4, 2026):** A round table organized by RX France (the event organizer) made this explicit — agentic AI will force enterprises to "explicate their internal rules." Those that haven't will face a governance crisis.

**Why this matters for Alizé:** This is the clearest possible market signal. The governance gap identified in Alizé's context engineering brief (June 5) is now being discussed at Snowflake Summits and Big Data & AI Paris. The problem Alizé is being built to solve — "who can do what, with which data, through an AI agent, and on whose authority" — is becoming a mainstream enterprise concern. Alizé's three-module positioning (context audit trail, context guardrails, context compliance scoring) maps directly onto what these enterprises are starting to feel they need.

## Finding 3: French AI Funding — Thin Slice of a €202M Week

**Source:** Maddyness MaddyMoney, June 5, 2026

This week's French startup funding round-up (Maddyness, June 5):
- **Total raised by French startups:** €202 million
- **AI-specific startups:** 2 startups raised a combined **€8.6 million**
- **Context:** This is a small fraction of total funding — the majority going to other sectors (notably insect farming/agritech with Innovafeed's €51M raise)

**What this means:** AI funding in France is not having a moment. While the US and China continue to pour billions into foundation model companies, French AI startups are competing for a relatively modest share of domestic venture. This has implications for the Alizé business model: French SMBs are price-sensitive, and a managed AI governance service needs to demonstrate ROI clearly. The good news: when AI spending is constrained, governance and efficiency gains from proper context management (avoiding token waste) become more attractive.
