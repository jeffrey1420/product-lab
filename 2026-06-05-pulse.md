## EU AI Act: The Ticking Clock

**The August 2, 2026 deadline is ~58 days away.**

Companies deploying AI agents in EU AI Act Annex I critical infrastructure sectors (energy, transport, banking, healthcare, digital infrastructure) are now operating **high-risk AI systems** — whether they know it or not.

Key obligations for high-risk systems under the Act:
- Registration in EU database before going live
- Conformity assessment (self or third-party depending on sector)
- Technical documentation (Articles 11-12)
- Human oversight measures
- Transparency to users
- Data governance compliance

**The gap:** AI vendors (including Mistral) are not proactively flagging this to their enterprise customers. Companies that deployed Vibe Work Mode in the past 6-12 months are likely non-compliant and don't know it.

**Alizé opportunity:** The compliance bridge doc (alize-vibe-workflow-compliance-bridge.md) is the right framing. This is a real, time-sensitive pain point with a concrete deadline. A packaged compliance + deployment service for French enterprises in regulated sectors is a credible, differentiated offer.

## Emerging Themes

1. **Sovereign AI infrastructure is no longer theoretical.** Mistral has the capital, compute, and enterprise traction to be the European default. Alizé's "we deploy and manage Mistral/Vibe agents for you" positioning rides this wave.

2. **Context engineering is the next bottleneck.** Enterprise AI deployments are hitting cost and accuracy walls from context overflow. Tools like Trace AI (YC W26, $3M seed) are emerging to solve this via knowledge graph-based task decomposition. Alizé could incorporate context scoping into its managed service offering.

3. **Agent governance is unclaimed territory.** Most agent platforms (Mistral, OpenAI, Anthropic) focus on capability. Governance, compliance, audit trails, escalation protocols — these are secondary. Alizé can own this space for French enterprises that need more than a raw agent API.
