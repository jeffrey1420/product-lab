## Signal 1: Mistral Workflows — Public Preview Since April 27, 2026

This is the most significant new signal this cycle. Mistral's **Workflows** product (mistral.ai/news/workflows, April 27, 2026) is an enterprise orchestration layer that directly addresses the "proof of concept to production" gap. This is relevant to Alizé both as a competitive pressure and as validation of the market need.

**What Workflows does:**
- Durable execution: tracks state at every step, resumes from failure point (not restarts)
- Full observability: every branch, retry, and state change recorded in Studio
- Human-in-the-loop: single line `wait_for_input()` pauses workflow for approval — resumes where it stopped
- Enterprise connectors: ships with pre-built integrations
- Python-authored workflows: published to Le Chat so anyone in the org can trigger them
- Production deployment in "days" from POC (Mistral's claim)

**Production customers named:**
ASML, ABANCA, CMA-CGM, France Travail, La Banque Postale, Moeve

**Use cases documented:**
- Cargo release automation (customs, dangerous goods, multi-jurisdiction regulatory checks)
- KYC document compliance checking (identity extraction, sanctions/PEP verification, structured risk assessment)
- Customer support triage (routing, intent categorization, correctability at workflow level)

**Why this matters for Alizé:**
Mistral is building the production orchestration layer. This validates the market need. Alizé's differentiation must be the **regulatory compliance layer on top of Workflows** — EU AI Act conformity documentation, Article 12 audit log requirements, Article 14 human oversight, Article 16 serious incident reporting to national authorities. Mistral builds the engine. Alizé makes it legally deployable in French critical infrastructure.

## Signal 2: Mistral Forge — Enterprise Custom Model Training

Mistral Forge (mistral.ai/news/forge, March 17, 2026) lets enterprises train frontier-grade models on their proprietary knowledge. Pre-training on internal docs/codebases/operational records. Reinforcement learning aligned to internal policies and operational objectives.

**Enterprise partners using Forge:** ASML, DSO National Laboratories Singapore, Ericsson, European Space Agency, HTX Singapore, Reply

**For Alizé:** Forge customers are training models on proprietary data and deploying them in enterprise workflows. When those Forge-trained models run inside Workflows, the combined system is itself a high-risk AI system under EU AI Act. Forge + Workflows deployment = Alizé's exact target scenario. The conformity assessment requirement falls on the deploying enterprise, not Mistral.

## Signal 3: Vibe Work Mode Architecture — L2 Adaptive Collaborator Confirmed

The Vibe agent announcement (mistral.ai/news/vibe-agent, May 28, 2026) describes Work Mode behavior precisely:

> "It maps out a plan and gets your sign-off before it starts, then works across your connectors to carry the task through."

This is textbook **L2 Adaptive Collaborator** behavior (agentic.ai framework: 12–17 points). Vibe Work Mode:
- Plans multi-step goals
- Gets human approval before execution
- Works across connectors (email, calendar, Slack, GitHub, databases)
- Sessions persist and can run on schedule (daily/weekly/monthly)

**Pricing on announcement page:** Free, Pro ($14.99/month), Team ($24.99/month) — no mention of Enterprise pricing (custom)

**Key detail:** "Skills" allow reusable workflows via open standards. This is Mistral's answer to MCP for workflow portability.
