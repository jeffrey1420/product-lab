## What EU AI Act Requires (for Annex I critical infrastructure)

**For AI systems operating in:** energy, transport, water, healthcare, finance, public administration, digital infrastructure

| Article | Requirement | Status in Vibe Work/Workflows |
|---------|-------------|------------------------------|
| Article 11 | Inventory of all AI systems in the company | Your responsibility |
| Article 12 | Technical documentation (conformity assessment) | Your responsibility |
| Article 13 | Logging and audit trail requirements | Partially provided (Studio) — insufficient alone |
| Article 14 | Human oversight framework ("meaningful human control") | Provided by design — but must be documented |
| Article 15 | Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity | Your responsibility |
| Article 16 | Serious incident reporting (48h to national authority) | Your responsibility |
| Article 43 | Conformity assessment before market deployment | Your responsibility |
| Article 72 | Penalties: up to €30M or 6% global turnover | Your risk |

**You get none of the documentation, classification, or reporting infrastructure from deploying Vibe Work or Workflows.**

## Objection Handling

**"We don't have critical infrastructure AI"**
→ Ask: "Are you using Vibe Work or Workflows in energy, water, transport, healthcare, finance, or public administration?" If yes, EU AI Act Annex I applies. Document classification is required.

**"Mistral handles compliance"**
→ Mistral provides a platform. The conformity assessment, incident reporting protocol, and CNIL documentation are the deploying company's legal responsibility. Mistral's enterprise agreements explicitly state this.

**"We have an IT department"**
→ EU AI Act compliance for high-risk AI systems requires legal, compliance, and technical documentation — not just IT configuration. Your IT team configured Vibe. Alizé documents the compliance.

**"We don't have the budget"**
→ The fine for non-compliance is up to €30M or 6% of global annual turnover. The compliance engagement is a fraction of that risk.
