## Signal #1: The Agentic AI Hype Cycle Has Arrived — But Enterprises Are Still Doing Workflows

### What's Happening

"Agentic AI" is everywhere. The term now has its own dedicated site (agentic.ai, updated June 3, 2026), its own Inria explainer (March 2026), MIT Sloan coverage (February 2026), and PwC France publication (September 2025). The message from all of them: AI that acts autonomously is the next step.

But here's what the hype cycle obscures: **most enterprise AI deployments in 2026 are still workflows, not agents.**

The agentic.ai site defines it precisely — agentic AI pursues goals through its own actions, plans steps, calls real tools, observes results, and loops. This is a spectrum from "mildly agentic" (web search) to "highly agentic" (managing a sales pipeline without supervision).

Inria frames it as a "functional rupture" rather than a scientific breakthrough — a new way for users to express intent ("gère mes mails cette semaine" instead of "rédige un mail").

PwC calls it "Agentic IT" — a paradigm shift where AI has genuine autonomy over IT processes.

**All of this is true. And all of it is currently priced into the top 5% of enterprise deployments.**

### What French SMEs Are Actually Doing

Based on what Alizé's positioning documents describe:

- Invoice processing: sequential extraction → validation → routing → archiving — a **workflow**
- Customer inquiry routing: classify → dispatch — a **workflow**
- Contract review: parallel LLM reviews → aggregation — a **workflow**
- Vulnerability analysis (Mythos use case): scan → human review → patch — a **workflow with human-in-the-loop**

None of these require an agent that dynamically directs its own processes. All of them benefit from AI capability. The distinction matters commercially:

| What buyers think they want | What they actually need | Alizé delivers |
|---|---|---|
| "An AI agent that handles everything" | Predictable, bounded AI workflow | Workflow design + deployment |
| "Cutting-edge autonomous AI" | AI that doesn't make unexplained decisions | Governance layer + audit logs |
| "The latest AI technology" | Measurable ROI on a specific task | Per-workflow KPIs and reporting |
| "AI that works like a human" | AI that augments specific human tasks | Task-targeted workflow deployment |

### The Risk for Alizé

If Alizé gets painted as "not agentic enough," it loses deals to vendors selling the narrative. The answer is not to claim agentic capabilities Alizé doesn't have. The answer is to reframe: **agentic is high-risk, high-cost, and high-governance burden. Most use cases don't need it. Alizé delivers the outcome without the risk.**
