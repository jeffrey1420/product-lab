## Signal #1: Mistral Rebrands Le Chat → Vibe (June 1, 2026)

### What Happened

Mistral AI officially retired "Le Chat" and launched "Vibe" at the AI NOW Summit. The rebrand is not cosmetic — it signals a fundamental shift in Mistral's strategic ambition.

Key facts:
- **Vibe Chat:** Conversational AI, free tier, inherits Le Chat's position
- **Vibe Work:** Enterprise agent platform connecting to Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, email — analyzing data, drafting reports, automating workflows. €14.99/mo Pro, €24.99/mo Team
- **Vibe Code:** Developer agent in CLI, web UI, and VS Code extension
- **Skills:** Reusable multi-step workflow automations — Mistral's answer to Claude's tool use patterns
- **Infrastructure:** €4B invested, 3 data centers (40MW in France), scaling to 1GW by decade's end
- **Model:** Powered by Mistral Medium 3.5

### The Strategic Signal

"Le Chat" was a French chatbot — approachable, local, a little charming. "Vibe" is a global productivity platform. The rename means:

1. **Mistral is going enterprise.** Vibe Work's integration with enterprise tools (Workspace, Slack, GitHub) is a direct enterprise sales motion. This is no longer a consumer/free tier story.

2. **Vibe Work is Alizé's direct competitor.** Mistral is now selling workflow automation to French businesses. Same target segment. Same product category. The question is: does Mistral offer governance, compliance, and operational oversight? Based on current public info: no. Vibe Work is a tool. Alizé is a managed service with governance.

3. **Mistral is not providing EU AI Act compliance documentation.** Vibe Work customers deploying in critical infrastructure (energy, water, healthcare, communications) face the same Annex III obligations as any other AI deployer. Mistral has not published EU AI Act conformity documentation.

4. **Skills are workflow templates.** Mistral's "Skills" feature — reusable multi-step automations — is exactly the "workflow pattern catalog" that Alizé's BRIEF.md describes. This validates the workflow taxonomy but also means Mistral is moving into this space aggressively.

### Alizé Angle: Vibe Work Creates a New Compliance Gap

Every French company that adopts Vibe Work for operational workflows (not just internal productivity) will eventually face:
- EU AI Act Article 11: inventory of AI systems in the company
- EU AI Act Article 12: audit logs of AI outputs
- EU AI Act Article 14: human oversight framework
- EU AI Act Article 72: serious incident reporting

Mistral provides the tool. They do not provide the compliance package. This is Alizé's opening.

**Pitch for Vibe Work customers:** "Vibe Work automates your workflows. We govern them. We handle your EU AI Act compliance so you can use Vibe Work without regulatory risk."

## Signal #2: Vibe Work EU AI Act Compliance Gap — Quantified

The 10h pulse established the 6-step compliance pathway for critical infrastructure AI. Vibe Work adds a new dimension: Mistral's enterprise customers are deploying AI workflows right now, without any compliance infrastructure.

The gap is not theoretical. French companies using Vibe Work for:
- Invoice processing workflows
- HR document handling
- Contract review pipelines
- Customer service automation

...are all running high-risk AI systems under Annex III if they operate in critical infrastructure sectors. They need:
1. An AI system inventory (Article 11)
2. A conformity assessment (Article 43)
3. Human oversight documentation (Article 14)
4. Audit logs (Article 12)
5. Incident reporting procedures (Article 72)

Mistral offers none of this. Alizé offers all of it.
