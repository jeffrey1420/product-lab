## Signal 5: Cloud Exit Trend — AI is Accelerating On-Premise Adoption

A Maddyness op-ed by Thomas Thelliez (CTO of Positive Group) argues that AI is breaking the "all-cloud" dogma. Key points:
- AI workloads are making cloud dependency visible and expensive
- Companies are rediscovering on-premise/hybrid strategies for cost control, data sovereignty, and long-term sustainability
- The question is no longer "cloud vs. on-premise" but "what can we afford not to control?"
- This shift is particularly acute in France/EU due to GDPR and the desire for data sovereignty

**For Alizé:** The hybrid/sovereign AI narrative is getting stronger. Alizé's positioning around French/EU data governance fits perfectly into this trend. Enterprise clients increasingly want AI that runs in a controlled, auditable environment — that's Alizé's governance layer.
