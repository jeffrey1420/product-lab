## Top Story: EU AI Act Omnibus — The Compliance Clock Just Reset

The EU institutions reached a provisional political agreement on the **Digital Omnibus on AI** on May 6, 2026 (confirmed by Member States on May 13). Formal publication in the Official Journal is expected before the original August 2, 2026 deadline.

**What changed:**

| System type | Original deadline | **New deadline** |
|---|---|---|
| Stand-alone Annex III (recruitment, credit scoring, law enforcement, education, border control) | Aug 2, 2026 | **Dec 2, 2027** |
| Annex I — AI embedded in regulated products (medical devices, machinery, vehicles) | Aug 2, 2027 | **Aug 2, 2028** |

**What did NOT change — August 2, 2026 remains active:**
- **Transparency obligations (Article 50)** — must disclose to users when they are interacting with an AI system
- **Watermarking obligation** — existing systems get a grace period until Dec 2, 2026

**Strategic implication for Alizé:**
The "August 2 deadline = scramble" narrative that the compliance bridge doc leaned on is now out of date. The high-risk obligations are 17–29 months away. This is NOT a fire drill anymore — it's a planning horizon.

This is actually **bullish for Alizé's managed service model**: enterprises now have more time to do AI right, not just fast. The differentiator shifts from "we'll help you scramble" to "we'll help you build something that actually works and is ready when the deadline arrives."

**Recommended messaging pivot:** Stop leading with urgency. Lead with the managed service value proposition — we design, deploy, and operate your AI workflows so you can focus on your business. Compliance readiness is a natural byproduct, not the core pitch.

## Market Signal: Enterprise AI Agents Are Still Early — The Gap Is Infrastructure, Not Models

McKinsey 2025 data cited by OneReach.ai: **92% of enterprises plan to increase AI spending over the next three years. Only 1% feel they've achieved true AI maturity.**

The bottleneck is not model capability — it's organizational infrastructure:

1. **Data fragmentation:** Enterprises run an average of **897 applications**; only **29% can interface with each other**. When systems don't talk, AI agents lack context and make poor decisions.

2. **Integration complexity:** 60% of organizations cite legacy system integration as their primary AI scaling challenge (Deloitte 2025). Enterprise systems were designed for human operators, not autonomous agents.

3. **Governance vacuum:** 73% of enterprises want explainable, accountable AI — but most lack the governance frameworks to oversee agents operating at scale.

**For Alizé:** This validates the managed service positioning. Building a workflow is the easy part. Connecting it to fragmented enterprise systems and running it with proper governance is where the real value lives. Alizé's offer maps directly to the #1 and #3 pain points.
