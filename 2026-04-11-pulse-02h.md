## 4. France Ditches Windows for Linux — European Tech Sovereignty Accelerating

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/10/france-to-ditch-windows-for-linux-to-reduce-reliance-on-us-tech/)

France announced plans to migrate government computers from Windows to Linux, driven by desire to "regain control of our digital destiny." Minister David Amiel cited data control and digital infrastructure independence. This follows France dropping Microsoft Teams for French-made Visio (Jitsi-based). France also plans to migrate health data platform to a "trusted platform" by end of year.

The broader European Parliament voted in January to identify areas to reduce reliance on foreign (primarily US) tech providers.

**What this means for Alizé:**
- European tech sovereignty is now mainstream government policy — Alizé's European positioning is validated
- French government actively migrating away from US software stacks = market opportunity for European AI agents
- If France is dropping Microsoft Teams, French companies may drop Microsoft 365 + Copilot in the next 2-3 years
- Alizé should consider "European data residency + EU AI Act compliance" as a key differentiator vs US-based AI agents
- The sovereign cloud angle (Outscale, OVHcloud, Scaleway) could be part of Alizé's technical architecture
