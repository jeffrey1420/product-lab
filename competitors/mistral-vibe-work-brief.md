## Overview

Mistral AI launched "Vibe" on June 1, 2026, retiring the "Le Chat" brand entirely. Vibe is a full enterprise AI platform with three tiers: Vibe Chat (consumer/free), Vibe Work (enterprise workflows, €14.99-24.99/mo), and Vibe Code (developer tools). This brief focuses on **Vibe Work** — the product most relevant to Alizé's positioning.

**Bottom line:** Vibe Work is a workflow automation platform. It does not provide EU AI Act compliance infrastructure, governance, audit trails, or regulatory oversight. Every French company deploying Vibe Work in critical infrastructure sectors needs what Alizé provides. This is not a coincidence — it is the opening.

---

## Vibe Work: What It Does

Vibe Work connects to enterprise tool ecosystems (Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, email) and automates workflow tasks:
- Data analysis and report drafting
- Email management and response
- Workflow automation across connected tools
- Business process automation

**Pricing:**
- Free: Vibe Chat tier
- Pro (€14.99/mo): Vibe Work access
- Team (€24.99/mo): Team features + higher limits

**Infrastructure:** Mistral has €4B invested across three data centers, 40MW in France, scaling to 1GW. This is serious infrastructure — Mistral is not a startup playing small.

## The Compliance Gap: Why It Matters

The EU AI Act Annex III designates AI systems used in critical infrastructure as high-risk. Vibe Work customers in these sectors — energy, water, healthcare, communications, transport — face mandatory compliance obligations:

1. **Article 11:** Inventory of all AI systems in the company
2. **Article 43:** Conformity assessment before going live
3. **Article 14:** Human oversight framework for every AI system
4. **Article 12:** Automatic, tamper-resistant audit logs
5. **Article 72:** Serious incident reporting to national authorities

Vibe Work's French enterprise customers are deploying high-risk AI systems today. Mistral is a French company. Mistral has not published EU AI Act compliance documentation for Vibe Work. This is not a criticism — Mistral is building an AI platform, not a compliance consultancy.

**Every Vibe Work customer in a critical infrastructure sector is exposed.**

## Alizé's Opening: The Vibe Work Compliance Bridge

**Pitch:** "Vibe Work automates your workflows. Alizé makes them EU AI Act compliant. We handle the governance, audit trails, and regulatory documentation so you can deploy Vibe Work without regulatory risk."

This positioning:
- Does not require Vibe Work customers to switch platforms
- Addresses a real, immediate compliance gap
- Positions Alizé as additive rather than competitive
- Is credible: the EU AI Act obligations are real and ENISA is watching
