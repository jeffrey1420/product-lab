## Signal 2: EU AI Act — French SME Compliance Reality (August 2, 2026)

The morning pulse covered the enforcement timeline. This cycle: a deep-dive on what French SMEs actually need to do, based on keerok.tech's French SME compliance guide.

### The Compliance Gap for French PME

French authorities (CNIL, HAS, ARCOM, DGCCRF) are **delayed in designation** — only 8/27 EU member states had designated national competent authorities by August 2025. This delay doesn't exempt companies: **documenting compliance efforts now protects you in future audits.**

### What Every French SME Must Do Before August 2026

**Step 1: Inventory and classify AI systems**
- List every AI tool: SaaS, APIs, in-house ML, automations
- Classify each: unacceptable / high-risk / limited-risk / minimal-risk
- High-risk examples: CV screening, credit scoring, insurance client scoring, recruitment automation
- Limited-risk: chatbots (must disclose AI nature)
- Deliverable: Excel/Airtable catalog of all AI systems + risk classification

**Step 2: Document governance and human oversight (HIGH RISK only)**
- Article 14 requires: no critical decision without human supervision
- Article 11 requires: technical documentation for every high-risk AI decision
- Actions needed: designate AI officer (can be outsourced), draft AI governance policy, document automated decision criteria, implement human review gates

**Step 3: Assess and mitigate bias (HIGH RISK)**
- Article 10: training data must be free of discriminatory bias
- Audit datasets used to train/feed AI systems
- Document mitigation steps

**Step 4: Register high-risk systems in EU database**
- Required before August 2, 2026 for high-risk AI systems
- Registration via national authority (CNIL for France, pending full designation)

### The Alizé Opportunity

The SME compliance market is wide open. Most French PME don't know:
1. They likely have high-risk AI systems already (CV screening in HR SaaS, client scoring in their CRM, automated invoice processing)
2. They're legally required to document human oversight by August 2, 2026
3. The fine is up to €35M or 7% of global turnover — not a rounding error

**Alizé's compliance offering should be:**
> "On inventory vos systèmes IA, on met en place la supervision humaine et la tracabilité, on vous rend conformes avant août 2026."

This is actionable, time-bound, and maps directly to Alizé's existing workflow governance capabilities.
