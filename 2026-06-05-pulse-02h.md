## Signal 1: Anthropic IPO Filed June 1, 2026 — $900B Valuation

This is the most significant AI industry event since the Mistral Vibe rebrand. Anthropic confidentially filed an S-1 with the SEC on June 1, 2026. Financial Times reporting indicates:
- **Valuation:** ~$900B (potentially $965B in some reports)
- **Raise:** ~$30B in new capital
- **ARR:** $47B (from prior reporting)
- **Status:** Confidential filing — IPO timeline not yet public

**Strategic significance:**

Anthropic going public changes the competitive dynamics in several ways:

1. **Increased regulatory scrutiny** — A public AI company will face SEC oversight, shareholder pressure, and heightened expectations around AI safety disclosures. This will push the entire industry toward more rigorous governance standards.

2. **"Your AI vendor is going public" pitch angle** — French enterprises using Claude via API or enterprise contract will face harder questions from their legal and compliance teams. "Anthropic is now a public company — regulators will scrutinize every AI deployment. Is yours documented for EU AI Act compliance?"

3. **Mythos EU initiative gains importance** — Anthropic's Mythos EU (dedicated EU AI Act compliance framework) becomes a selling point for French enterprises. But Mythos EU covers Anthropic's obligations — not the deploying enterprise's obligations under Articles 12–16.

4. **IPO creates sales urgency** — When Anthropic's S-1 goes public and the roadshow begins, French decision-makers will read about Claude in the financial press. This is a top-of-mind moment for AI governance. Alizé should position itself as the compliance partner for companies that just read about Claude in the FT.

**New Alizé pitch angle this cycle:**

> "Anthropic filed for a $900B IPO on June 1. When a company that size goes public, regulators pay attention. ## Signal 2: Automation Anywhere "Autonomous Enterprise" — Imagine 2026

Automation Anywhere held its Imagine conference (May 19, 2026) and announced significant platform enhancements to its Agentic Process Automation (APA) platform. Key announcements:

**AAI Code — Enterprise Workflow Builder**
- Low-code tool for building enterprise-grade AI-driven applications
- Describe workflows in natural language or bring existing SOPs, diagrams, screenshots
- "Plans first, builds second" — designed for enterprise governance before production
- Ships complete UI, processes, agents, context, and security in ~1 week
- Key differentiator from "vibe coding" tools: enterprise-grade governance from day one

**Context Intelligence Graph**
- Part of the Process Reasoning Engine (PRE)
- Retrieves the *right* context for each workflow step — not all context
- Key insight: "In complex workflows, more context is not always better. Irrelevant information can slow execution, increase costs, expose sensitive data unnecessarily, and introduce inaccurate or inconsistent decisions."
- This is exactly the EU AI Act Article 11 (data governance) and Article 12 (technical robustness) challenge

**Universal Orchestration**
- Coordinates work across Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, custom applications, humans, AI agents, and automations within a single process
- This is the "AI agent coordination layer" — exactly the market Mistral Workflows is targeting

**For Alizé:** Automation Anywhere represents a mature competitor in the enterprise automation space with a strong governance narrative. The Context Intelligence Graph insight — "right context, not all context" — aligns with Alizé's EU AI Act data governance pitch. However, Automation Anywhere is RPA-centric, not AI-agent-native. The market is converging: every enterprise automation platform is adding AI agent capabilities.
